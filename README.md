# TransmogSweep

Every transmog appearance on the auction house you have not collected yet, cheapest first, right in the auction house window of WoW Forever. Then buy them one after another with a single button.

**Website:** https://bencepo.github.io/transmogsweep/

![TransmogSweep in the auction house](https://bencepo.github.io/media/transmogsweep/demo.gif)

- **Its own auction house tab.** The tabs of Auctionator, VendorFloor and other addons stay where they are.
- **Fast scan.** Asks the auction house for weapons and armor only, grouped by item: a few seconds, no 15-minute wait, repeat it any time.
- **Account-wide.** An appearance learned on any of your characters counts as collected. The list follows what the game itself shows in the item tooltip ("You haven't collected this appearance").
- **Unique appearances** (on by default): one row per look, the cheapest item that has it. Looks you already know through another item are left out. Turn it off for completionist mode: every item whose own source you lack.
- **Usable by my class** (optional): only armor types and weapons your current character can wear.
- **Filters:** search, slot, maximum price, hide items above your level.
- **Buying top to bottom.** "Start buying" checks the first item's price, the next click buys it, and after every purchase the next row is checked by itself. One click per item, nothing is ever bought without one.
- **Bought looks stay hidden** until you learn them: wear the item or sell it to a vendor.
- **Ctrl+click** previews an item, **right-click** hides a look you never want, **Alt+click** shows a diagnosis to copy into a bug report.
- **Settings window of its own** (gear in the tab or the addon menu at the minimap): drag it anywhere, changes apply live.

## Feedback and bug reports

For a wrong entry or a failed purchase, Alt+click the row and paste the diagnosis text. No GitHub account? Comment on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/transmogsweep#comments). Every report gets read.
